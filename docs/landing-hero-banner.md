## Final generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Premium horizontal hero banner background for Riverz ecommerce AI agent team, 1792x1024. LEFT 53% completely empty uniform cream #f3f0eb for live website headline. RIGHT half: a beautifully designed TEAM OF THREE DISTINCT AI AGENTS visibly collaborating. Portray them as three sophisticated geometric digital identities, NOT humans and NOT humanoid robots: each identity is a bold sculptural abstract four-petal or interlocking-loop emblem nested inside its own softly rounded vertical cream panel. Three agent panels arranged in an elegant triangular team formation, central/front panel slightly larger, two behind at left and right. Their emblems have distinct silhouettes but one cohesive identity system. Agent one features a tiny shopping bag and checkmark, agent two a tiny conversation bubble, agent three a tiny parcel/tracking symbol. Fine curved connecting lines and three small lime signal dots link the three panels, expressing coordinated intelligence. Precise polished editorial art direction, tactile matte paper relief and gentle natural shadows, restrained and premium rather than childish. Strict Riverz palette warm cream #f3f0eb, near-black petrol #12201f, muted sand #cdbca0, pale lime #f7ff9e accents. ALL key elements contained in x55%-95%, y18%-82%. No people, no faces, no humanoid robots, no letters, no words, no numbers, no logos, no fake text, no dashboard, no neon, no glossy chrome, no purple, no gradients, no watermarks. This must read visually as a connected TEAM of AI agents, not a stack of unrelated product feature icons. Background edges uniform cream. No headline: live text is added by the website.
