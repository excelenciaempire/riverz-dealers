# Riverz: propuesta de logo v1

Logo horizontal con monograma fluido, tipografía en minúsculas y colores verde oscuro y lima. PNG con transparencia real. Creado con la herramienta integrada de generación de imágenes. Archivo original conservado; no se reemplazaron los logos publicados.

## Prompt utilizado

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: primary brand logo for Riverz, a premium Spanish ecommerce AI company that coordinates customer conversations and automation.
Create one distinctive, exceptionally polished, minimalist logo, not a presentation board.
Composition: a compact symbol on the left and the wordmark on the right, one horizontal lockup, centered with generous clear space.
Exact text: "riverz" in all lowercase, spelled r-i-v-e-r-z. Custom refined medium-bold geometric sans serif, optical kerning, approachable rounded details with a sharp confident z, extremely readable.
Symbol: invent an elegant compact flowing lowercase r monogram from two interlocking continuous paths, expressing a river and coordinated movement. Strong silhouette that reads well at favicon size. A restrained lime accent integrated into one path. The mark should be ownable, contemporary, confident and slightly playful.
Palette: dark forest green #12201F for wordmark and most of symbol; luminous pale lime #F7FF9E as a small accent. Flat solid colors.
Background: genuine transparent alpha.
Style: pristine flat vector-like brand identity, crisp edges and balanced proportions, sophisticated technology brand.
Constraints: output only one finished symbol plus wordmark. No tagline, no labels, no board, no surrounding mockups, no watermarks, no shadows, no gradients, no 3D, no robot, no shopping cart, no generic speech bubble, no decorative elements. Do not copy existing logos.
